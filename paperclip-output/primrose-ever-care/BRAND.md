# Primrose Ever Care — Brand

## Identity
- **Name:** Primrose Ever Care
- **Tagline:** Compassionate home care in Medway
- **Tone:** Warm, professional, trustworthy
- **Audience:** Families seeking home care for elderly relatives in Medway

## Colours
- Primary: Pink (from logo — soft rose)
- Secondary: Soft blue
- Background: White / very light grey
- Text: Dark grey / near-black

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond (elegant, care-appropriate)
- Body: Clean sans-serif

## Voice Rules
- Warm and reassuring — families are often stressed. Language should calm.
- Professional but not clinical — avoid medical jargon
- Always mention care areas (Rochester, Chatham, Gillingham, Strood, Rainham)
- CTA language: "Book a Free Care Assessment" — not "Contact Us"
- Never use pushy sales language
- Mention CQC registration process where trust signals are needed

## Services (always reference correctly)
- Personal Care
- Dementia Care
- Companionship
- Medication Support
- Live-in Care
