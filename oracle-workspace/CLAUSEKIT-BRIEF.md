# ClauseKit — Full Product Brief

## What It Is
AI contract builder for UK freelancers and small businesses. No UK equivalent exists — Contractable proves the model in the US (500+ contracts/week).

## What It Does
Plain English input → AI-generated bespoke UK law contract → PDF + Word download → pay to unlock

## Stack
- Next.js 14 (App Router) + Tailwind CSS
- OpenAI GPT-4o for contract generation
- Stripe for payments
- pdf-lib or react-pdf for document generation
- Vercel for deployment

## User Flow
1. Homepage → "Create a contract" CTA
2. Pick contract type OR free text input
3. Conversational intake — 5-8 questions (parties, work description, payment, special clauses, dates)
4. AI generates contract with loading state (15-30 seconds)
5. Preview in browser — clean formatted document
6. First page visible free, rest blurred — prompt to pay
7. Pay £7 via Stripe → unlock full PDF + Word download

## MVP Contract Types (8)
1. Freelance/project agreement (web, design, marketing)
2. NDA (mutual + one-way)
3. Retainer agreement
4. Subcontractor agreement
5. Client service agreement
6. Website terms & conditions
7. Late payment / debt recovery letter
8. Basic employment offer letter

## UK Legal Layer (every contract)
- English/Welsh law by default
- Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act 1998 reference on relevant contracts
- IP ownership: freelancer retains until paid in full (UK default)
- GDPR data processing note where applicable
- IR35 awareness flag on freelance contracts
- Disclaimer footer: "Generated by AI. Not legal advice. Consider review by a solicitor."

## Monetisation
- Pay-per-doc: £7 per download
- Unlimited plan: £19/mo
- No account needed to generate — sign up to download

## Target Users
UK freelancers, web/design/marketing agencies, consultants, small businesses
