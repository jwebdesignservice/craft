09 Revision Policy

This Agreement includes 20 rounds of revisions per project phase. Revisions are defined as minor adjustments to existing design or content — not new design directions or additions to scope.

Additional revisions beyond those included will be charged at $50 per hour and agreed in writing prior to commencement.

All revision requests must be submitted in writing and consolidated into a single document per round.

10 Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full and final payment, the Client will be granted full ownership of the completed design and development work, including all source files and assets created specifically for this project.

The Designer/Developer retains the right to display the completed work in their portfolio and for promotional purposes, unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

Any third-party assets (stock images, fonts, plugins, frameworks) remain subject to their respective licences. The Designer/Developer is not liable for the Client's misuse of third-party licenced materials.

11 Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 7 days' written notice.

In the event of termination by the Client, the deposit is non-refundable. Any work completed up to the point of termination will be invoiced at the agreed hourly/pro-rata rate and must be settled within 14 days.

In the event of termination by the Designer/Developer, any unused portion of fees already paid will be refunded, less the value of work completed to that point.

12 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary or sensitive information shared during the course of this Agreement. This includes but is not limited to business strategies, customer data, pricing, and technical systems.
