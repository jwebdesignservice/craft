02 Project Overview

Project Name: Primrose Ever Care — Website Design & Development

Project Description:

Design and development of a full professional website for Primrose Ever Care LTD, a domiciliary home care business operating in the Medway area (Rochester, Chatham, Gillingham, Strood, Rainham). The site will reflect the business's compassionate, professional brand and serve as the primary digital presence for attracting care clients and building trust.

Primary Goals & Objectives:

Generate enquiries from families seeking home care in Medway · Build brand credibility and trust · Clearly communicate services offered · Achieve strong local SEO rankings for Medway care-related searches · Comply with all regulatory requirements for a CQC-registered care business
03 Scope of Work

The following services are included within the scope of this Agreement:

  • Homepage — 9-section layout (hero, trust bar, services overview, why choose us, areas covered, testimonials, CTA, contact, footer)
  • Services Page — individual detail sections per service: Personal Care, Dementia Care, Companionship, Medication Support, Live-in Care
  • About Page — company story, values, aims & objectives, team section, CQC registration status
  • Areas We Cover Page — Google Maps embed, area-specific content for Rochester, Chatham, Gillingham, Strood, Rainham
  • Contact / Enquiry Page — 2-step enquiry form (About You & Care Requirements), Google Maps embed
  • Legal Pages — Privacy Policy, Cookies Policy, Complaints Procedure, Safeguarding Policy
  • Form Email Integration — contact form wired to client inbox via email service
  • Cookie Consent Banner — GDPR compliant (required by UK law)
  • Click-to-Call Button — mobile-prominent tap-to-call functionality
  • SEO Optimisation — meta tags, titles, descriptions targeting Medway care search terms on all pages
  • Fully Responsive — optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • Deployment — hosted and live on Vercel with custom domain connection

Any work falling outside the agreed scope will be discussed and agreed upon in writing before commencement, and may incur additional fees.
